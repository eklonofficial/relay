🏎️ HexGL (3D Racing)

A futuristic anti-gravity racer in the style of WipEout, by Thibaut Despoulain (MIT License). Click Start, then click again to begin. ↑ accelerate, ←/→ steer, A/D airbrakes. Three laps — beat your time. Loads about 18 MB the first time you open it.

Vendored from github.com/BKcore/HexGL (MIT). Analytics removed — see docs/games/hexgl/CREDITS.md.

🎯 Shooter (3D FPS)

A modern three.js first-person shooter with Bullet physics and AI enemies, by Mohsen Heydari (MIT License). Click NEW GAME, then click the view to capture the mouse. WASD move, mouse aim, left-click shoot, R reload, Space jump, Esc releases the mouse. Loads about 46 MB the first time you open it.

Built from github.com/mohsenheydari/three-fps (MIT). Asset credits and changes in docs/games/three-fps/CREDITS.md.

🔫 Gladiator 3D (FPS)

A first-person raycaster shooter (the "STFP" demo of the Gladiator 3D engine by Matthias Ladkau, MIT License). Vendored into this repo and served from this site — no external calls. Click the game first, then: ↑/↓ move, ←/→ turn, Space to shoot. Press Space on the title screen to start.
